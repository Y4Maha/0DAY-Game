TAGLINE (≤60 chars):
A card game where every card is a real cyberattack or defense

DESCRIPTION (≤260 chars):
Build a deck of 8. Outsmart your opponent across 3 target systems in a 3-minute match. Every card mirrors a real attack or defense — phishing, brute force, MFA, honeypots. Free, browser-based, no signup.

MAKER COMMENT (post on launch):
Hi PH. I built 0DAY in 3 weeks as a demo to test a thesis: can a card game teach real security concepts as a side-effect of play, not as a lesson? After each match, one screen surfaces what just happened in real-world terms (e.g., "Phishing → Stolen Creds → Brute Force starts 36% of breaches"). Educational, but only if you want it to be. Looking for honest feedback from gamers and security folks both.
